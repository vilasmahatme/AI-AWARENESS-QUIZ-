SECTION B – GENERATIVE AI CONCEPTS
_________________________________________________________________________________________

11. What does LLM stand for?
Answer: Large Language Model

12. What is Generative AI?
Answer: AI that creates new content such as text, images, audio, or video

13. What is a prompt in AI?
Answer: A prompt is an instruction or input given to an AI model

14. What is a system prompt?
Answer: A system prompt defines the AI's behavior, role, and rules

15. What does multimodal AI mean?
Answer: AI that can understand or generate multiple types of data, such as text, images, audio, and video

16. What is an AI hallucination?
Answer: An incorrect or fabricated AI-generated answer that may appear convincing

17. What are tokens in an LLM?
Answer: Tokens are small pieces of text processed by an LLM

18. What is an AI model's context window?
Answer: The maximum amount of information an AI model can consider in one interaction

19. What does inference mean in AI?
Answer: Inference is the process of using a trained AI model to generate an output

20. What is fine-tuning?
Answer: Fine-tuning is training a pre-trained model further on specific data for a particular task

21. What is an AI agent?
Answer: An AI agent is a system that can plan, use tools, and take actions to achieve a goal

Answer: Model Context Protocol

Answer: An API key is used to authenticate and authorize access to an AI service

26. What is an API rate limit?

27. What type of AI product is Cursor?

28. What is NotebookLM mainly designed to help users work with?
Answer: Documents and other source materials for research, understanding, and summarization

29. What is semantic search?

41. What is an open-weight AI model?
Answer: An AI model whose trained weights are publicly available for others to use or modify, subject to its license

42. What is model distillation?
Answer: A technique for training a smaller model to learn from a larger model

43. What is LoRA commonly used for?
Answer: LoRA is commonly used to efficiently fine-tune large AI models

44. What does MoE stand for in AI models?
Answer: Mixture of Experts

45. What is an AI benchmark?
Answer: A standardized test used to measure and compare AI model performance

46. What is a guardrail in an AI application?
Answer: A rule or safety mechanism that controls AI behavior and reduces harmful or unwanted outputs

47. What is prompt injection?
Answer: An attack that tries to manipulate an AI system through specially crafted instructions

48. What does PII stand for?
Answer: Personally Identifiable Information

49. Why should confidential company data not automatically be pasted into public AI tools?
Answer: It may expose sensitive information and create privacy or security risks

50. If an AI-generated answer looks convincing, what should a user do before using it for an important decision?
Answer: Verify the information using reliable sources before making the decision
